# 1Zpresso grind-setting research (fetched 2026-09-07)

**Source hierarchy:** (1) 1Zpresso official — grind-setting page table, per-model reference chart images, user manuals, calibration page, FAQ; (2) Honest Coffee Guide (HCG); (3) blogs: Coffee Chronicler, coffeemaster.app, Barista Life, Kaffebox.

### Conventions

- **Notation R.N.T** = Rotations . Numbers . Ticks(clicks). Total clicks from zero = R × clicks/rotation + N × clicks/number + T.
- Official charts for J-Max, J-Ultra, X-Pro, X-Ultra carry a cumulative click axis (exact). Official charts for K-Plus/K-Max/K-Pro, K-Ultra and ZP6 use a "Point" axis = cumulative dial numbers (10 clicks each). JX-Pro and Q charts use cumulative dial numbers (4 and 3 clicks per number). Band edges read off images to the nearest half-number; treat as ±2–3 clicks.
- **µm per click is burr-gap travel**, not particle size.
- **"S" models** differ only by the foldable handle; settings identical ( https://1zpresso.coffee/faq/ ).
- All are **hand grinders, stepped**.

Official adjustment table: https://1zpresso.coffee/grind-setting/

| Model group | Adjustment | Numbers/rot | Clicks/rot | Clicks/number | mm per click |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q series, J, JE | Internal | 10 | 30 | 3 | 0.025 |
| JX-Pro S, JE-Plus | Top | 10 | 40 | 4 | 0.0125 |
| X-Pro S, X-Ultra | External | 6 | 60 | 10 | 0.0125 |
| J-Max S | External | 9 | 90 | 10 | 0.0088 |

### 1. JX-Pro / JX-Pro S
- Burr 48 mm stainless conical. Top dial (inside hopper), 10 numbers/rot, 40 clicks/rot, 4 clicks/number. 12.5 µm/click. Official chart spans 5 rotations = 200 clicks.
- Zero: "where the handle starts to spin with resistance instead of the tightest position"; dial should read #0 ±2 clicks ( https://1zpresso.coffee/manual-jxpro-en/ , https://1zpresso.coffee/calibration/ ).

| Moka | 2.4–3.0 rot | 96–120 | 79–144 |

### 9. ZP6 Special

### Cross-cutting caveats

1. **Zero point tolerance is model-specific:** JX-Pro ±2 clicks; J-Max and J-Ultra ±10 clicks; K-Plus/K-Max/K-Pro/ZP6 and K-Ultra "two or more clicks"; Q series: zero is the tightest position, the dial number there "might not be 0".
2. **Zero definition differs by family:** internal-dial Q/J/JE = tightest position; top and external designs = where resistance starts.
3. **Factory alignment:** "do not turn the handle within the first few clicks from the zero point" ( https://1zpresso.coffee/faq/ ).
4. **Official charts never list Chemex or cold brew** and group AeroPress + Moka + drip into one band and siphon + pour over into another.
5. X-Pro and X-Ultra charts are pixel-identical; K-Plus/K-Max/K-Pro and K-Ultra charts have identical Point ranges despite different µm/click.
