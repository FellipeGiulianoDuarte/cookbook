# AeroPress recipe research report (compiled 2026-09-07)

Sourcing note: primary = the author's or manufacturer's own page. Secondary = recipe aggregators (aeroprecipe.com, aeromatic.app) or blogs that transcribe the primary.

## 1. James Hoffmann, "The Ultimate AeroPress Technique" (Episode #3)

- **Author / year**: James Hoffmann, YouTube, April 2021 (video ID j6VlT_jUVPc).
- **Source URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j6VlT_jUVPc ; transcriptions: https://aeroprecipe.com/recipes/james-hoffmann-aeropress-recipe , https://klaire.codes/coffee/coffee/james-hoffman-aeropress/ , https://www.fluentincoffee.com/james-hoffmann-aeropress-recipe-what-he-actually-does/
- **Dose**: 11 g. **Water**: 200 g; ratio 1:18.2
- **Grind**: "finer end of medium" (finer than pour-over). Coarsen for darker roasts.
- **Temperature**: light roast: boiling / 99-100 °C, used immediately. Transcriptions: medium roast 90-95 °C, dark roast 85-90 °C.
- **Orientation**: standard (upright). **Filter**: 1 paper micro-filter, not rinsed. Brewer not preheated.
- **Steps**: 0:00 add 11 g coffee, pour 200 g water, start timer. ~0:10 insert plunger about 1 cm to make a vacuum that stops drip-through. 2:00 gently swirl the whole assembly (no stirring). 2:30 press gently, about 30 s. ~3:00 done.
- **Notes**: aeromatic.app lists 95 °C; treat as an aggregator's choice.

## 2. Alan Adler original recipe (inventor)

- **Source**: current official instructions https://aeropress.com/pages/how-to-use . Historical Adler recipe: https://honestcoffeeguide.com/brew-recipes/original-aeropress-recipe/ , https://aeroprecipe.com/recipes/alan-adler-original , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AeroPress
- **Dose**: 1 scoop "fine drip grind". Grams disagree: aeroprecipe 15 g; Honest Coffee Guide 15-20 g; current official site 1 heaping scoop = 16-18 g.
- **Water**: fill to mark 1 to 1.5 (≈90 g). Ratio ≈1:6 before dilution.
- **Grind**: fine ("between espresso and drip").
- **Temperature**: 80 °C / 175 °F. Official FAQ: 175 °F (80 °C) for dark roasts, 185 °F (85 °C) for medium and light: https://help.aeropress.com/en-US/why-do-you-recommend-brewing-with-175%C2%B0f-(80%C2%B0c)-water-for-dark-roasts-and-185%C2%B0f-(85%C2%B0c)-for-medium-and-light-roasts-123203
- **Orientation**: standard. **Filter**: 1 paper.
- **Steps**: add coffee; pour water to mark 1-1.5; stir 10 s; insert plunger; press gently 20-40 s. **Dilution**: "add hot water to your tasting" (americano-style). **Total**: ~1:00-1:30.
- **Current official instructions (aeropress.com/pages/how-to-use)**: 1 heaping scoop or 16-18 g; medium (pre-ground) or medium-fine grind; 185 °F (85 °C) or boiling; fill to mark **4**; stir **3 s**; steep 60 s (medium) or 30 s (medium-fine); press with gentle constant pressure until the hiss ends. XL: 2 scoops (32-36 g), fill to mark 8. Go: "slightly less coffee".

## 3. Tim Wendelboe AeroPress

- **Source (primary)**: https://timwendelboe.no/pages/how-to-brew-with-an-aeropress
- **Dose**: 14 g, light roast. **Water**: 200 g; ratio 1:14.3
- **Grind**: "fine filter grind" (secondary: "medium, 15-20 clicks Comandante")
- **Temperature**: primary page says **"boiling temperature"**. Secondaries say 96 °C.
- **Orientation**: standard. **Filter**: 1 paper, rinsed under tap ~10 s.
- **Steps**: 0:00 pour 200 g; stir 3 times; place plunger on top to stop draining; 1:00 remove plunger, stir 3 times front-to-back; press with body weight into a large cup. "Don't over press." **Total**: ~1:30.

## 4. World AeroPress Championship winning recipes, 2018-2025

Official source: https://worldaeropresschampionship.com/pages/recipes (mirror https://aeropress.com/blogs/w-a-c-aeropress-recipes ). Doses are 18 g from 2021 on (competition standard).

### 2025: Némo Pop (Australia)
- Dose 18 g. Brew water 100 g at 84 °C; bypass 70 g at 50 °C (final ~170 g). Water 125 ppm.
- Grind: Comandante Trailmaster x25, 31 clicks, **sifted at 200 µm** (fines removed).
- Orientation: **upright**. Filter: **Flow Control Filter Cap + 2 paper filters**.
- Steps: pour 70 g bypass water into carafe; place AeroPress on carafe; add coffee; pour 100 g brew water wetting all grounds; **0:25** stir "NSNS-WEWE"; **0:50** start pressing gently, ~20 s; serve. Total ~1:10-1:20.

### 2024: George Stanica (Romania)
- Dose 18 g. Water 85-90 ppm, 96 °C for extraction; dilution with warm water + 0 ppm room-temp water.
- Grind: Comandante C40 Mk4 Red Clix, 58 clicks (**870 µm** stated).
- Orientation: **inverted**, plunger at ~4th mark. Filter: 1 Aesir paper, rinsed.
- Steps: 0:00 pour ~50 g via Melodrip (5-6 s); bloom 30 s; pour another 50 g (5-6 s); stir NSEW 10 s; rinse filter; **1:20** cap, purge air ~10 s; swirl, place on server; **1:35** press gently 30-40 s to ~76-79 g output; dilute with warm water to 130-135 g total, then add 20-30 g room-temp 0 ppm water. Total ~2:15.

### 2023: Tay Wipvasutt (Thailand)
- Dose 18 g (16 g + 2 g added mid-brew). 89 °C, 100 g brew. Grind 1Zpresso ZP6, 65 clicks. Inverted, 1 paper rinsed.
- Steps: 0:00 add 16 g, pour 100 g; 0:30 stir 5 s; 0:45 add 2 g coffee; 0:55 stir 5 s; purge air, cap; 1:35 flip, press ~30 s (yield ~75 g); bypass room-temp water to 115 g; bypass hot water to 155 g. Total ~2:05.

### 2022: Jibbi Little (Australia)
- Dose 18 g. 90 °C; 94 ml brew; bypass at 90 °C to 150 g total. Target TDS 1.3-1.35 %. Grind: Timemore (coarse) + Kinu at 4, sifted. Inverted, 1 paper rinsed.
- Steps: pour 94 ml; stir 35 times gently; 1:20 cap, press out air; 1:30 flip; 1:40-2:10 press 30 s; yield 58-64 g concentrate; bypass to 150 g. Total ~2:10.

### 2021: Tuomas Merikanto (Finland)
- Dose 18 g. Third Wave Water Formula 2, 200 g at 80 °C. No bypass. Grind Macap Labo 70D, setting 7.0. Inverted, 2 paper, rinsed.
- Steps: 0:00-0:10 pour 50 g; 0:10-0:15 stir gently 3x; 0:15-0:30 pour to 200 g; 0:30-0:50 wait; 0:50 stir gently 3x; 1:00 press air out, cap; 1:35 pitcher on; 1:40 flip; 1:40-2:00 press; 2:00-2:30 swirl to cool. Total 2:30.

### 2019: Wendelien van Bunnik (Netherlands)
- Dose 30 g. Spa Blauw (30 ppm) at 92 °C; 100 g brew; +100 g water bypass. Grind Mahlkönig EK43S 7/10. Inverted, Aesir paper rinsed.
- Steps: pour 100 g in 10 s; stir firmly 20 times in 10 s; cap, press out air; 0:40 flip and press all coffee (~60 g concentrate); add 100 g water; cool to ~60 °C. Total ~1:00 to press.

### 2018: Carolina Ibarra Garay (USA)
- Dose 34.9 g. Brita-filtered, 85 °C; 100 g brew; +60 g at 85 °C and +40 g room temp (200 g total). Grind EK43S 8/10. Inverted, paper rinsed.
- Steps: pour 100 g over 30 s; stir vigorously with chopsticks 30 s; cap, flip, press 30 s into glass server; top up 60 g hot + 40 g room temp. Total ~2:00.

**Pattern across 2018-2025**: 7 of 8 winners inverted; 7 of 8 use a concentrate + bypass; temperatures 80-96 °C; brew-phase contact 0:40 to 1:40; press 20-40 s.

## 5. Standard vs inverted

**Standard (upright)**, per official instructions: paper in cap, cap on chamber, chamber on mug; add coffee; add water; stir 3 s; steep 30-60 s (or insert plunger slightly to make a vacuum and stop drip-through); press until hiss. Pros: stable, no flip, faster. Cons: drip-through starts immediately unless the plunger vacuum trick or a Flow Control cap is used.

**Inverted**, per https://perfectdailygrind.com/2020/10/the-inverted-aeropress-method-a-guide/ : plunger on counter, seal up; chamber pushed onto plunger so the seal is **at least 20 mm** inside; add coffee and water; stir/steep; wet filter, screw cap on; put mug upside down on cap, grip chamber and plunger together, flip in one confident motion; press. Pros: zero drip-through. Cons: extra steps, unstable stack. **Safety**: top-heavy and full of near-boiling water; a shallow seal or hesitant flip spills. Do not invert over thin glass. AeroPress itself does not publish an inverted method; the Flow Control Filter Cap is the manufacturer's answer to drip-through.

## 6. Two recipe families

**A. Concentrate + bypass ("americano-style")**: small water volume, high dose, short contact, then dilute in the cup. Adler original; every WAC winner 2018-2025 except 2021; official XL Americano (30 g fine grind, 100 g at 79 °C, Flow Control cap closed, stir 10 s, open and press slowly, add 300 g water; https://aeropress.com/blogs/aeropress-recipes/the-xl-americano ).

**B. Full-immersion, single-strength**: whole beverage brewed at drinking ratio (1:14 to 1:18), no dilution. Hoffmann 11 g / 200 g; Wendelboe 14 g / 200 g; official current 16-18 g to mark 4; Coffee ad Astra 18 g / 260 g / 99 °C / **10-minute steep** with swirls at 5:00 and 9:00, gentle 1-minute press, paper filter on top of a Prismo metal disk (https://coffeeadastra.com/2021/09/07/reaching-fuller-flavor-profiles-with-the-aeropress/ ).

## 7. Filters

- **Standard paper micro-filter** (https://aeropress.com/blogs/blog/a-complete-guide-to-filters-for-your-aeropress ): holds back grounds, most oils and fines; light body, clean cup. XL needs XL-size paper.
- **Two paper filters**: "increases absorption" for an even clearer cup; slightly slower flow. Used by 2021 and 2025 winners and the official Flash Brew.
- **Rinsing**: Hoffmann: no rinse. Wendelboe and most WAC winners: rinse. Official instructions: no rinse step.
- **AeroPress stainless steel filter**: **178 µm** holes; oils and fines pass, fuller body.
- **Able Disk** (https://ablebrewing.com/products/disk-coffee-filter ): Standard **254 µm** holes, fuller body; Fine **152 µm**, cleaner and sweeter, bends easily.
- **Fellow Prismo** (https://fellowproducts.com/products/prismo ): pressure-actuated valve plus **150 µm** disk; no drip-through, so standard orientation replaces inverted. Not compatible with Go, XL. Espresso-style dial-in: 20 g espresso-fine, 50 g boiling water, pour 10-20 s, stir 20-30 s, press hard, 10-20 s press, ~60 s total.
- **AeroPress Flow Control Filter Cap** (https://aeropress.com/products/aeropress-flow-control-filter-cap ): valve; no drip until pressing; **not** XL.

## 8. Grind size in microns

- **Honest Coffee Guide**: AeroPress **320-960 µm**; espresso 180-380, moka 360-660, V60 400-700, pour-over 410-930, French press 690-1300.
- **Ue Coffee Roasters** (cites HCG): short 1-2 min standard brew **400-600 µm**; espresso-style under 1 min **~320 µm**; long 3-4 min steep **800-960 µm**.
- **WAC official page**: 2024 winner 870 µm; 2025 winner sifted out fines below 200 µm.
- **Rule for the app**: grind follows contact time. Concentrate/short-steep (under ~1:00): 300-450 µm. Standard 1-2 min immersion: 400-600 µm. Long steep (3-10 min) or bypass style: 700-950 µm.

## 9. Water temperature

- **Official AeroPress**: 80 °C dark, 85 °C medium and light. Current instructions also allow boiling.
- **Hoffmann**: light 99-100 °C, medium 90-95 °C, dark 85-90 °C.
- **Wendelboe**: boiling (primary) / 96 °C (secondary).
- **Honest Coffee Guide** (https://honestcoffeeguide.com/best-temperature-to-brew-coffee/ ): AeroPress 90-95 °C; light 90-96, medium 87-95, dark 82-93.

## 10. Models

Official comparison https://aeropress.com/pages/compare-aeropress : Original, Clear, Premium, Go Plus: 10 oz (~295 ml); Go: 8 oz (~237 ml); Original XL and Clear XL: 20 oz (~590 ml), XL-size filters. Go: use slightly less coffee; 12-15 g / 180-225 g typical. XL: double dose and water; press 30-60 s longer; practical dose 40-50 g.

## 11. Iced / Japanese-style flash brew

- **Official "Japanese Iced Coffee"** (AeroPress, 2024-10-24, https://aeropress.com/blogs/aeropress-recipes/the-ultimate-guide-to-making-japanese-iced-coffee-with-aeropress ): 22 g medium-fine; 200 g water at 99 °C to mark 3; 150 g large ice cubes in the glass; upright; stir 10 back-and-forth; insert plunger and pull up slightly to stop drips; steep ~3:00; press 30-45 s.
- **Official "Iced AeroPress Flash Brew"** (2024-04-16, https://aeropress.com/blogs/aeropress-recipes/iced-aeropress-flash-brew ): 20 g medium-fine; 200 g at 99 °C; 100 g ice in carafe; upright; 2 paper filters or Flow Control cap; stir 10-15 times; steep ~3:00; press 30-45 s; serve over fresh ice.
- **Hoffmann iced** (secondary, https://aeroprecipe.com/recipes/james-hoffmann-iced-aeropress ): 22 g; 240 g at 100 °C; 160 g ice in decanter; upright, plunger inserted; wait 4:00; swirl; wait 0:30; press; total ~4:30.

## 12. Roast and process adjustments

- **Roast**: see section 9; grind coarser for darker roasts (Hoffmann). Official AeroPress: 80 °C dark vs 85 °C medium/light.
- **Process** (weakly sourced): https://www.indiancoffeebeans.com/learn/aeropress-indian-coffee-recipe : washed 15 g / 210 ml / 90-93 °C / medium-fine / steep 1:30 / press 20-25 s; natural 15 g / 210 ml / 84-88 °C / medium / steep 1:45-2:00; anaerobic 14 g / 196 ml / 80-84 °C / medium-coarse / steep 1:15. No guidance from Hoffmann, Wendelboe, AeroPress, or Barista Hustle on washed vs natural was found; present as "community guidance".
